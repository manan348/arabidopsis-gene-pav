The table shows all three methods for every accession.

Read call: normalised depth (MAPQ ≥ 10) and breadth from mosdepth. Delly: share of the gene inside a PASS deletion. Liftoff: found = intact at its own place; partial = less than half aligned; elsewhere = only a look-alike far away; missing = not placed. GO groups come from enriched terms (FDR < 0.05); a gene can belong to several, the first listed wins. Generated by scripts/make_pav_explorer.py.
